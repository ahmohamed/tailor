# Your standing rules

`/tailor` reads this file before every selection and applies each rule as if you said it in the session. `/setup` seeds it; add to it whenever you make a ruling you want to stick. Examples of what belongs here:

- **Framing rulings** — how you describe an employer or a role ("describe X as a Silicon Valley financial institution").
- **Named exclusions** — projects or documents that must never appear in a CV (conflicts of interest, relinquished IP, sales documents).
- **Rendering conventions** — e.g. "one simple title per role: render the current title for the whole tenure", or how a relocation is shown in the header.
- **Selection biases** — e.g. "for AI roles, always bring the published ML work alongside the LLM work".
- **Visibility policy** — which employers/clients/projects must stay unnamed and be described functionally only.
